Meet Your Mentor

Global Impact Strategist, Entrepreneur, and Founder of World Changers

Egbodofo Joshua

Global Impact Strategist & Founder

From Young Entrepreneur to Global Impact Leader

Egbodofo Joshua is not your typical mentor. His journey began at the remarkable age of 16, when most teenagers are still figuring out their future, Joshua was already building and scaling multi-million Naira businesses. This early start gave him a unique perspective on entrepreneurship, resilience, and the power of intentional action.

With extensive experience in the mining sector, Joshua has led large-scale gold mining projects and operational management, understanding the complexities of building businesses in challenging environments. His expertise spans from traditional industries to cutting-edge technology startups, making him uniquely qualified to guide entrepreneurs across multiple sectors.

Today, Joshua is actively building and scaling innovative technology startups while dedicating his time to mentoring the next generation of world changers. His mission is simple yet profound: to help intentional individuals move from passive aspiration to active, high-leverage desire that creates lasting impact.

Hands-On, Personalized, and Results-Driven
