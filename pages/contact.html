Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers to Common Questions

How long is the World Changers program?

The initial commitment is 3 months with a 4-week core curriculum. However, the frameworks and community support are designed for a 2-year growth journey.

What is the time commitment required?

You should plan for weekly mentorship sessions (1-2 hours), daily implementation work, and participation in the online community. Most successful participants dedicate 5-10 hours per week to the program.

Is the program conducted online or in-person?

The program is primarily conducted online, making it accessible to participants globally. Mentorship sessions, coursework, and community interactions are all virtual.

What if I'm not currently running a business?

That's okay! The program is designed for intentional individuals at various stages - from those with just an idea to established entrepreneurs. What matters most is your commitment to growth and impact.
