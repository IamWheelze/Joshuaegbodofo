Before You Apply

The World Changers program is highly selective and designed for intentional individuals who are committed to making a real impact. Before submitting your application, please ensure you meet the following criteria:

  • ✓ You are genuinely committed to personal and professional transformation
  • ✓ You can dedicate time each week for mentorship sessions and program activities
  • ✓ You are building or planning to build a business with ethical, impactful goals
  • ✓ You are ready to move from aspiration to action
  • ✓ You are willing to engage actively with the World Changers community
